# Terminology

## 40 Key Controls
Also means 1KF. Mentioned in some old HD forum posts.

## Deep Drop
Refers to teleporting pieces to their lowest possible placements regardless of the board state.

## Tuck Drop
Refers to teleporting pieces to their lowest possible placements with regular guideline controls and SRS (Super Rotation System).

## Top Drop
Refers to placing pieces without automatic tucking or deep dropping.
